Questions

Good to know

Where do you film?

Based in Marrakech, we film across the city, the Agafay desert, the Atlas Mountains, Essaouira and anywhere in Morocco — and travel abroad for projects. Travel beyond Marrakech is listed separately in the quote.

How much does a film cost?

Every project is quoted on its own: the days or half-days on location, the size of the crew and what you want delivered. You get a written quote with a clear total, and a 50% deposit confirms the dates.

Can you provide photography and video together?

Yes. For weddings and events we can send a photographer and a videographer side by side, so you get stills and film from every moment with one team and one invoice.

Can you live stream our event?

Yes. We run multi-camera live production with real-time switching and on-screen graphics, streamed to YouTube, Vimeo, LinkedIn, Zoom or a private link.

Do you use drones?

Yes, with a licensed pilot. Flying in Morocco needs authorisation, so tell us early and we handle the request.

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings, as early as possible: spring and autumn dates fill first. For events and brand films, two to four weeks ahead is ideal, but shorter notice often works — message us with your dates.
