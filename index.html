The studio

Based in Marrakech, shooting wherever the story lives.

EcoFrames Vision is a small, hands-on crew built around a single belief: every project deserves its own cinematic language. The name says it — each film carries its own echo, its own frame, its own clear vision.

From the colour and chaos of the medina to the stillness of the Atlas and the geometry of the desert, we shape light and place into films that feel made only for the people in them. One studio, end to end — from first call to final grade.
