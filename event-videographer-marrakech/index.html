Questions

Event video FAQ

Do you film events outside Marrakech?

Yes. We regularly follow groups to the Agafay desert, the Atlas Mountains and Essaouira, and we cover events anywhere in Morocco. Travel beyond Marrakech is listed separately in the quote.

Can you provide photography and video together?

Yes. For most corporate events we send a photographer and a videographer side by side, so you get stills and film from every moment with one team and one invoice.

Can you live stream our conference?

Yes. We run multi-camera live production with real-time switching and on-screen graphics, streamed to YouTube, Vimeo, LinkedIn, Zoom or a private link.

Do you use drones at events?

Aerial shots are available with a licensed drone pilot. Flying in Morocco needs authorisation, so tell us early and we handle the request.

How far in advance should we book?

Two to four weeks ahead is ideal, especially in spring and autumn. Shorter notice often works too: message us with your dates.

How do you price event coverage?

By the number of half-days or days on site, the size of the crew and what you want delivered: recap film, reels, photos or a live stream. You get a written quote with a clear total, and a 50% deposit confirms the dates.
